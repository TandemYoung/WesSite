Meet Wes

A lifetime of service to Arkansas.

Wes Freeman is a lifelong Arkansan, a Pope County small business owner, and a dedicated advocate for the people of the River Valley. Through his work with the Arkansas REALTORS® Association, Wes has spent twenty years fighting to protect Arkansans' private property rights and defend our conservative values at the State Capitol.

He and his family have built their life in District 44, and Wes is running because he believes the people of Pope and Van Buren counties deserve a representative with the experience and conviction to get things done from day one.

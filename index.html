Meet Wes

A lifetime of service to Arkansas.

Wes Freeman is a lifelong Arkansan, a Pope County small business owner, and a professional negotiator with 25 years of experience getting deals done for Arkansas families. As 2025 President of the Arkansas REALTORS® Association — and a three-time President of the Arkansas Valley Board of REALTORS® — Wes has spent two decades at the State Capitol fighting to protect property rights and defend conservative values.

When disaster has struck, Wes has been there. He has been a tireless advocate for Arkansans — fighting to secure over $4 million in relief for Arkansas families displaced by tornadoes, floods, and severe weather across the River Valley. He has also invested in the next generation as a coach and mentor in his community.

Wes is running because the people of Pope and Van Buren counties deserve a representative with the experience and conviction to get things done from day one.

Arkansas Tax Credit: your gift can effectively cost you nothing.

Donations to Arkansas state candidates qualify for a state income-tax credit (Form AR1800) of up to $50 per individual or $100 per joint return. For most Arkansas filers, the credit effectively returns your first $50 — or $100 jointly — at tax time. (Non-refundable; applied against your Arkansas state income tax owed.)

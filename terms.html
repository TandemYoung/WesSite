SMS Terms & Conditions

Terms for receiving text messages from the Wes Freeman for State Representative campaign.

Effective date: May 15, 2026

Program Description

By opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from the Wes Freeman for State Representative campaign at the mobile number you provide. These messages may include campaign news and updates, event and rally invitations, volunteer and canvassing opportunities, voter information (such as registration deadlines, early voting, and election dates, times, and polling locations), and requests for support.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 6 messages per month, with more frequent messages in the days leading up to an election.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Message and data rates are determined by your wireless carrier and are your responsibility. The campaign does not charge for text messages.

How to Opt Out

To stop receiving text messages at any time, reply STOP to any message you receive from us. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, after which no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

How to Get Help

For help or more information, reply HELP to any message you receive from us, or contact the campaign at info@wesfreemanforstaterep.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers may change, and the program may not be available on all carriers.

Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. Information collected through the text-messaging program is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
